Customer Audit and Theft Detection

Energy accounting methods that locate commercial loss and theft.

The services provided are:

  1. Identifying Energy import and export points of area
  2. Estimating energy sales to customers on prorate basis when Meter readings are taken at different dates
  3. Methodology for proper accounting when an area is fed by two different sources during Audit period
  4. Computation of Distribution and AT&C loss
  5. Segregation of Energy loss as Technical, Commercial and Collection losses
  6. Break up of Commercial loss as energy Loss occurred due to utility short comings like poor quality metering, defective metering etc. and Energy Loss occurred due to Theft and malpractices of Customer like Meter tampering, Meter bypass & Directtapping of Lines. etc.
  7. Review of Energy sales pattern of MV customers and high value LV customers, who constitute 10% of customer population but account for energy sales
  8. Determining Bench marks of Commercial Loss for utility considering best practices
  9. Prepare a Commercial Energy Loss reduction plan to bench mark set over a period.
  10. Estimating cost & benefits of Energy loss reduction plan and performing financial analysis like IRR and NPV
  11. Estimating Impact of loss reduction plan on Corporate Balance sheet.
