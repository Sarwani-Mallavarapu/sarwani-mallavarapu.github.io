Data Centres

Requirement analysis, design, construction and commissioning of utility data centres.

All utilities in the world store and retrieve millions of customer records with asset information every day. Data Centre is a repository of data that helps analyse past & future trends and business potential. Diversified locations of asset information make storage, maintenance and retrieval a highly complex process and tremendously increase resource requirements. Consolidating the data at a single place, a data center, helps the utility optimise operations and improve productivity.

GECE provides services in the areas of

  • Requirement Analysis - Identifying and freezing client's performance criterion
  • Facility Design - Topology Analysis, Location Identification and Space Planning
  • Infrastructure Design - Designing required Electrical and Mechanical Systems
  • Technology Design - Hardware, Software and Communication Systems Design
  • Modelling - Develop multiple Snapshots of current & future scenarios
  • Detailed Design - Detailed specifications on Mechanical, Electrical and Technology
  • Security - Fire Protection, Physical Security, Electrical and Mechanical Failures
  • Infrastructure Development - Physical construction and implementation of Data Center
  • Operations - Deployment of software solutions and commissioning of Data Center

We have a track record to reckon with, in building data centres for utilities. We can Build your customer and asset databases from scratch or migrate existing data in a risk free environment. Compliance with Electricity Regulatory requirements is our hallmark in building data centres. We have successfully established data centers for power utilities in India and Africa.
