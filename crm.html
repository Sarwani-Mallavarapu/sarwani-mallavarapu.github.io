Customer Relationship Management

Contact-centre, location-based and mobile CRM for utility customers.

In the information technology era, the customer may not visit your premises physically, but would be visiting electronically. If you do not provide him the service in time and does not stick to the promised service levels, he may not reconnect to you again.

GECE services in respect of CRM is more of a combination of strategies, technologies and best practices adopted in managing and analysing the satisfaction and future needs of customer by frequent interactions throughout the customer lifecycle for retaining the customer. CRM strategy is built on platforms of website, telephone, direct mail, mobile Apps and of course social media. CRM software strategy consolidates customer information and documents into a single CRM database so business users can more easily access and manage it.

Contact Center CRM strategy is designed to reduce tedious aspects of a contact center agent's job, including pre-recorded audio that assist the customers in problem-solving and information dissemination. The software tools of the company integrate with the utility officials desktop to handle customer requests to simplify customer service processes.

Geo-location-based services strategy is to locate the customers’ physically on the GIS map, which can also be used as a networking with customers based on location.

On-premises CRM where the software resides on utilities own servers and fully integrate with company’s data.

Mobile Apps based CRM strategy is to develop CRM based on the applications developed on mobile platforms. This would enable many customers to utilise the CRM from any where and everywhere and at any point of time

The company is in the process of developing cloud-based CRM leveraging its robust domain and industry expertise in power sector -- On-demand CRM. Our CRM would enable the utilities of future to work on cloud based CRM which will be cost-effective and service oriented.

GECE has implemented CRM solution in integration with GIS successfully for a leading power utilities in 5 towns in Western India and in eastern part of India catering to total of 1000000 customers. A single applications with contact center, Location based services deployed On-premises CRM has been deployed by GECE for many of its clients in India
