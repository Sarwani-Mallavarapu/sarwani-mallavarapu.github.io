AT&C Loss Reduction

Estimation and reduction of aggregate technical and commercial losses.

AT&C loss is the difference between the amount of electricity received by a Distribution Company from the Transmission Company and the amount of electricity for which it invoices its customers plus the adjusted collections loss

Total losses have two components: technical and non-technical. Technical losses occur naturally and consist mainly of power dissipation in electricity system components such as distribution lines, transformers, and measurement systems. Non-technical losses are caused by actions external to the power system and consist primarily of electricity theft, non-payment by customers, and errors in accounting and record-keeping.

Technical Losses Reduction

GECE Developed in-house state-of-art Software for Estimation and Reduction of Technical losses of ill conditioned Networks prevailing in developing countries. Technical loss is estimated by load flow analysis taking into consideration GIS database, MV feeder meter Readings at substation, DT meter readings and Customer Energy sales every month. This gives most accurate estimation of Technical (Energy) Loss.

The following Technical loss reduction measures are simulated:

  1. Network Reconfiguration
  2. Conductor size upgradation
  3. Express feeder or Bifurcation feeder
  4. Placement of shunt capacitors
  5. Placement of Voltage Boosters/ Regulators
  6. Upgrading Voltage of feeder
  7. Conversion of OH feeder as UG cable feeder.

Loss verification and certification studies are carried out on completion of project to determine actual loss reduction achieved compared to what is projected. The study has shown that actual reduction is more than what was projected.

Commercial Loss Reduction

The services provided are:

  1. Break up of Commercial loss as energy Loss occurred due to utility short comings like poor quality metering, defective metering etc. and Energy Loss occurred due to Theft and malpractices of Customer like Meter tampering, Meter bypass & Direct tapping of Lines. etc.
  2. Review of Energy sales pattern of MV customers and high value LV customers, who constitute 10% of customer population but account for energy sales
  3. Determining Bench marks of Commercial Loss for utility considering best practices
  4. Prepare a Commercial Energy Loss reduction plan to bench mark set over a period.

Commercial losses or non-technical losses can be reduced using many techniques:

  • Technological solutions like Automation of Meter Reading Billing and Collection Systems; Technical solutions like converting LVDS to HVDS
  • Social solutions like educating the consumers and increasing awareness on the community loss due to theft, non-payment, etc.
  • Political solutions like removing subsidies, waiving tariffs, etc.

AT&C Reduction Plan aims at

  • Estimating cost & benefits of Energy loss reduction plan
  • Performing financial analysis like IRR and NPV
  • Estimating Impact of loss reduction plan on Corporate Balance sheet.
