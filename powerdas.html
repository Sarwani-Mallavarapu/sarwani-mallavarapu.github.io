POWERDAS®

POWERDAS® is a workstation providing automation functions comprising network operation, outage management and network automation and switching.

Ask about POWERDAS®All products
Modules

11 modules in POWERDAS®.

Part of the POWER suite of workstations, available off the shelf and customised to the requirements of each utility.

01

State estimator

Validation of data and load demand at each bus of the network.

02

Power supply monitor

Monitoring the status and loading of equipment and lines.

03

Reliability and quality indices

Computation of reliability and voltage indices.

04

Voltage stability

Evaluation of stability indices of each bus.

05

Optimal power flow

Optimisation of tap positions.

06

Fault detection

Identifies the faulty section through fault sensors.

07

Fault isolation and supply restoration

Addresses the problem of power supply restoration.

08

Emergency load shedding

Identifies the circuit feeders that have to be tripped to shed load.

09

Load balancing

Determines the configuration of the radial distribution network.

10

Integrated Volt-VAR control

Control of substation power transformers, feeder voltage regulators and feeder capacitors.

11

Load control

Controlling of HT and bulk loads to match the system load curve with optimal system capability.

Benefits

What POWERDAS® delivers for the utility.

  • Centralized monitoring of power supply to the area
  • Quick restoration of power supply to the healthy network
  • Prevention of power system collapse in the event of major outages
  • Improved reliability and better customer service
  • Minimisation of power losses
  • Better voltage profile
  • Optimal reactive power management
  • Control of peak loads and load balancing
  • Better utilization of assets
  • Quick fault detection and effective management of outages
