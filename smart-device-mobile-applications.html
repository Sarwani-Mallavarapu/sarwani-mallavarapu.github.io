POWERMOBILE®: Smart Device and Mobile Applications

Android applications that take the POWER workstations to field level operations.

GECE developed a suite of POWER workstations providing Network management, Network Operations and Customer management services deploying state-of-art technology. These POWER workstations are customized fully to meet the requirements of Utilities in developing countries. Mobile applications are provided to support the POWER workstations for Field level operations.

m-SURVEY

m-SURVEY is android application based Mobile Technology. The features of m-SURVEY are:

  1. Capture spatial location and Attribute data of Network Assets
  2. Validate attribute data as captured
  3. Display assets as surveyed on Google map or base map
  4. Build Network topology as survey proceeds and display network configuration
  5. Customer Indexing, duly Verifying customer account number entered matches with customer Billing database.

m-SURVEY Application has a module which will check for integrity of data downloaded from GPS rovers, build network topology and populate GIS database. Integrate customer Master database and network database to identify power supply route for each customers' dynamically.

m-PROJECT

m-project facilitates detailed field engineering of new Network projects formulated/ planned and on-line supervision & monitoring progress of projects. The project formulated by design Engineer on server is transmitted to field engineer Tablet for detailed Engineering along with network map, back ground map. The field Engineer walks along the lines proposed and captures actual location of poles and other line assets like DT, capacitor considering physical constraints and also check for ease of erection and maintenance. The field data captured is transmitted to server and displayed. The Proposal is finalized with detailed engineering design. Facility to take photograph of asset location and transmit it to server for storing in database is feasible.

When the Project is sanctioned and work Order is issued by competent authority, the bill of quantities, the WBS (Work Breakdown Structure) under each task are defined in server.The BOQ, and WBS with program dates is communicated to tablet of field engineer for inspection and recording the progress WBS wise on Tablet. This progress is transmitted to server to be stored in database and available for all stake holders on Project Portal.

m-ASSET

m-Asset Mobile application comprises a suite of Android applications to be loaded on tablets that provides for

  1. Capturing attribute data of assets as received in stores and at site from suppliers.
  2. Facility to validate asset data captured with reference to master tables and customized validation rules
  3. Receiving Job orders for Asset scheduled maintenance, Un-scheduled maintenance and Outage management.
  4. Inspection of assets and Record all the activities carried out in the field on tablet during Maintenance and outage management of assets.
  5. Show path on map to reach an asset if asset spatial coordinates are specified.

m-OUTAGE

This application automates field operations to be carried out on devices identified under outage during Trouble Call Management and Outage Management to restore power supply to customers.

DCC (Distribution Control Center) creates a job order on the outage device either in POWERTCM® or POWEROUTAGE®. This job order is communicated to TCWF (Trouble Call Work Force) team on Tablet for carrying out breakdown operations and restore power supply.

TCWF team proceeds to work spot, inspects outage devices and reports to DCC the damages that have occurred to assets, rectification work to be carried out to restore normal conditions and the time required. Team also examines the feasibility of restoring alternate power supply to healthy sections of network and time by which the switchings can be completed. DCC examines the proposal by making necessary simulations and accords approval for operations.

m-NEW CUSTOMER

This is a mobile application which automates all field activities relating to New Customer Management and is fully integrated with web application POWERCUSTOMER®

1. Inspection of Customer installation and capturing spatial location of new assets to be installed for release of Power supply to customer considering ground constraints. The load data of new customer is also collected during inspection.

2. Communicating approved work order along with the works proposed for implementation to the Mobile /Tablet of the Contractor and Field Technician.

3. Capturing new assets added using Mobile / Tablet, on completion of work to update GIS database.

m-CUSTOMER BILLING

The mobile application provides for two modes of Bill generation – Self Billing by customer and Billing by meter reader

Self Bill Generation by post paid meter Customers:This module generates new bill for month, when customer enters New Meter Reading in Mobile. It is mandatory for the customer to take photograph of meter to prevent him from committing the fraud of entering Low Meter reading. The meter reading and photograph is transmitted to server and stored in database. Server checks the meter reading entered for errors and sends message to recheck & confirm. On confirmation, Server prepares and transmits the Bill to be displayed on Mobile. Bill provides the complete details like current meter reading, previous meter reading, tariff category, energy charges, demand charges, arrears, Taxes & duties etc., Customer shall be able to print the bill, if desired.

BILLING BY METER READER: This module is similar to Self-billing by customer except for the fact that meter reader enters the reading and takes photograph in his Android tablet, in lieu of customer Mobile. The meter reader visits customer installation whose meter is to be read. The tablet conveys the Geo-location where meter reader is standing to server and the server in turn sends lists customers with account numbers, who are in vicinity. Meter reader selects the customer to be billed from the list. The bill is printed by meter reader using Bluetooth printer and handed over to customer.

m-CUSTOMER PAYMENT

This mobile application provides for four modes of bill payment –

  1. Self-Bill payment by postpaid customer
  2. Self Purchase of Energy by Pre paid customers
  3. Bill amount collection by Utility cashier
  4. Bill amount collection by Third parties like bank, Post office etc.

Bill Amount Payment by PostPaid Meter Customers: This module facilitates Customer can pay the bill amount using any one of the following modes Mobile service provider wallet; Net banking; Debit card; Credit card. The payment details are transmitted & stored in the server. A receipt is also generated and displayed on Mobile. If required customer can print receipt.

Bill Amount Collection by Utility Cashier: The application provides for online collection of Bill amount by utility cashier. The system must provides for four modes of payment namely Cash or Cheque or Debit Card or Credit card or Mobile wallet.

Bill Amount Collection by Third Party Agencies

This application shall facilitate on-line the bill amount collection by third party agencies like banks, post offices, commercial malls, etc., A tablet with Blue tooth printer shall be provided for executives from bank / post office / third parties. The customer approaches executive with bill issued by utility or with details customer account number & bill amount. Agency collects amount, enters the details in the tablet and delivers the receipt generated by server.

Purchase of Energy by Pre paid Meter Customers

The application provides the following facilities to pre paid meter customers,

  1. Pre paid customer can pay the energy purchase amount using his /her mobile or pay the amount to utility cashier or third party executive
  2. The amount paid by customer is transmitted to server by mobile phone or tablet
  3. Server will compute the energy that can be purchased with the amount paid, duly considering the tariff, previous purchases in the month.
  4. Server also will generate a receipt for amount received and energy purchased and transmit to tablet
  5. It also generates a key that need to be entered by customer in pre paid meter
  6. The customer will enter the key on the meter and meter updates the energy authorized for drawl

m-CUSTOMER CALL MANAGEMENT

This application provides facility to register all types calls received from customer Mobile phone. The customer calls can be broadly classified as Trouble calls and Service calls.

Trouble calls are the customer calls reporting outage of power supply to customer and calls reporting event /incidence that is likely to cause outage of power supply to utility customers, not necessarily the customer making call.

Service calls are all customer calls relating to meter reading, meter operation, billing, payment, staff service etc.,
