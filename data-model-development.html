Data Model Development

Data models that map utility business processes to information systems.

Data modeling is a process used to define and analyze data required for supporting the business processes by mapping the corresponding information systems in organizations. The data modelling professional working closely with business stakeholders, as well as present and future users of the information system.

GECE has systematically worked with many utilities in India and other developing countries where the computerization of the systems is preliminary stage or is non-existent. The business rules and processes are also not well defined and it is difficult for an information technology specialist to get an idea of the client’s requirements.

GECE has successfully developed a data model for the customer billing process for a client in South India

We have successfully developed a data model for a client in West Africa for their T & D computerization
