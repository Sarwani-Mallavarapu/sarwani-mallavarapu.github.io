Renewable Energy

Solar power system design and renewable grid integration studies.

Renewable Energy is generally defined as energy whose sources are naturally renewable on a daily basis. The best examples of renewable energy are Sunlight, wind, tides, waves, forest bio-mass and geothermal heat.

The most prominent among the renewable sources is the solar energy which is renewable, abundant, decentralized and non-polluting. It is available in tropical countries such as India, Middle East, Africa and Australia.

GECE has the experience and know how to design power system based on solar energy and its integration into the grid as per the norms.

Renewable Integration

Integrating the Renewable Energy and demand response of it into the electric distribution and transmission system is a focus area for energy consultants as the share of renewable energy in the total energy demand is picking up. A systems approach is being used to conduct integration development and demonstrations to address technical, economic, regulatory, and institutional barriers for using renewable and distributed systems. In addition to fully addressing various operational issues, the integration also establishes viable business models for incorporating these technologies into capacity planning, grid operations, and demand-side management.

GECE as a consultant performed the renewable energy integration project for an utility in South Asia, under a multilateral agency funding. The tasks undertaken are exhaustive and include

  • Design most appropriate settings and modes of individual Turbine Governors.
  • Review the current practice of Frequency Control and suggest the most suitable method of frequency control.
  • Derive appropriate settings and modes of Automatic Voltage Regulators (AVR) of the individual Generator.
  • VAR Scheduling of the power system with the combine operation of Generator AVR and Grid Transformer Tap control and infer suitable method of Voltage Control for the current and future power system
  • Methodology to determine how the frequency stability could be maintained with a higher proportion of non-centrally dispatched generators such as Wind power and Mini Hydro in the future system
  • Suggesting a proportion to be imposed to non-centrally dispatched power generators such as wind and Mini Hydro.
