Who we are

Measured by the results of our products and services.

Since our inception in 1995 we have been measuring our success by the results of our software products and consulting services. We help our clients make lasting improvements to their performance.

Our approach to traditional problem solving, through state-of-the-art technology solutions, has helped many utilities measure and manage risk and overcome the odds in accomplishing their stated objectives.

Our team of experts includes electrical engineers, software experts, financial experts, quality assurance specialists, legal advisors, regulatory and reform specialists, turnaround specialists and research scientists. Their diversity of background, discipline and outlook ensures our clients benefit from a balance of deep and proven expertise.
