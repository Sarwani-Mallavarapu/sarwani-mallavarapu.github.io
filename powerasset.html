Asset Management System

POWERASSET®

POWERASSET® is a comprehensive tool for the management of assets, their maintenance and outages. The tool is highly flexible and facilitates extensive user customization.

Ask about POWERASSET®All products
Modules

6 modules in POWERASSET®.

Part of the POWER suite of workstations, available off the shelf and customised to the requirements of each utility.

01

Asset inventory

Technical data of new assets.

02

Scheduled maintenance

Master data for scheduling maintenance.

03

Unscheduled maintenance

Recording all asset events, job card issue and closure.

04

Asset outage management

Outage status report, job card issue and closure.

05

Asset repair particulars

Entrusting a damaged asset to the repairer and recording the repairs carried out.

06

Special maintenance management

Inspection of the asset and recording of special maintenance carried out.
