Enterprise Asset Management

GIS-centric asset registries and maintenance management for utilities.

Asset management, broadly defined, refers to any system that monitors and maintains things of value to an entity or group. It may apply to both tangible assets such as buildings and to intangible concepts such as intellectual property and goodwill. Asset management is a systematic process of deploying, operating, maintaining, upgrading, and disposing of assets cost-effectively.

What’s unique to utilities, which distinguish them from many other industries needing Asset Management? Geographically distributed assets over varied expanses of topography and diversity in environmental factors that influence the same type of asset. GIS-centric Asset Management solution is the best-fit for utilities around the world. The biggest advantage of using GIS-centric asset management is standardized data that allows interoperability, providing users the capability to reuse, coordinate, and share information in an efficient and effective manner by making the GIS geo-database the asset registry.
