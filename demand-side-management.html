Demand Side Management

Energy efficiency measures that modify or reduce end-user demand.

Demand-side management is an application of energy efficiency measures that modify or reduce end-users’ energy demand. DSM is the implementation of those measures that help the customers to use electricity more efficiency

It is usually achieved through various methods such as financial incentives and consumer education.

DSM has been an area where GECE has many initiatives to claim its unique place. In majority of the projects undertaken by GECE, DSM constituted a part of the project. DSM encourages the consumers to use less energy during peak hours or to move the time of energy use to the off-peak hours viz. night.
