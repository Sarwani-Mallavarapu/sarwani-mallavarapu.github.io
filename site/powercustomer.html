POWERCUSTOMER®

POWERCUSTOMER® is a tool providing comprehensive new customer management. It monitors all activities from registration of the application to release of the new service connection.

Modules

6 modules in POWERCUSTOMER®.

Part of the POWER suite of workstations, available off the shelf and customised to the requirements of each utility.

01

New customer application

Manage your new service requests.

02

Utility job management

Identify utility requirements to release the connection.

03

Service call management

Manage all tasks and jobs until service commissioning.

04

Customer information system

Locate the customer and pull up customer details on the map.

05

Integration with GIS and asset databases

Integrate with GIS and asset systems to locate and manage utility equipment.

06

Enabling customer billing

Set up billing information, tariff structures and payment options.

Benefits

What POWERCUSTOMER® delivers for the utility.

  • On-line planning and design of supply arrangements for a new customer
  • Web-based customer services such as registration of the application and payment of security deposit and service charges
  • Quick release of power supply
  • Building up of the asset database
  • Accurate estimation of the cost of releasing supply
  • Investigation of alternate supply arrangements
  • Minimized time between release of supply and issue of the first bill
  • Updating of GIS showing the network in various stages
  • Electronic storage of customer documents such as the application, agreement and test report
