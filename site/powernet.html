POWERNET®

POWERNET® is a workstation providing advanced utilities to perform analysis, optimization and planning of distribution networks. IEEE compliant.

Ask about POWERNET®All products
Modules

9 modules in POWERNET®.

Part of the POWER suite of workstations, available off the shelf and customised to the requirements of each utility.

01

Analysis

Conduct load flow for any kind of network and simulate what-if scenarios.

02

Optimisation

Reconfigure and upgrade your network for minimal losses.

03

Planning

Forecast loads and configure least-cost network expansion plans.

04

Design

Mechanical and electrical design for lines, and earth mat design for substations.

05

Protection

Determine fault levels and configure relay coordination settings.

06

Reliability

Compute your reliability indices and simulate improvement measures.

07

Estimation

Speed up project cost estimation.

08

Evaluation

Financial analysis including NPV, IRR and payback period.

09

Appraisal

Quick and systematic economic and environmental analysis.
