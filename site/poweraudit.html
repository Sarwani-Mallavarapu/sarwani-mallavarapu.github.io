POWERAUDIT®

POWERAUDIT® is a power workstation for the estimation and allocation of total losses, and for load research, through accounting and audit of T&D facilities.

Ask about POWERAUDIT®All products
Modules

6 modules in POWERAUDIT®.

Part of the POWER suite of workstations, available off the shelf and customised to the requirements of each utility.

01

Feeder energy audit

Account for energy supplied at 11 kV and LT feeders and compute losses.

02

Transformer energy audit

Audit energy supplied at the distribution transformer and determine losses.

03

HT consumer feeder energy audit

Audit dedicated HT consumer feeders to identify any theft.

04

Customer audit

Review selected customers based on energy sales.

05

Customer class audit

Comparative review of a selected customer class.

06

Theft and court cases

Monitor all theft-of-energy cases, booked and pending.
