SCADA Automation

SCADA strategy, design and procurement, and integration with GIS, EMS and DMS.

Supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) systems play a vital role in delivering power in a reliable and safe manner to the customer and result in a range of operational improvements for the utility. The current market for SCADA systems is driven by the growing need for integration with other systems, communicating and sharing data across users and systems, using new communication and networking technology, economic reasons like profit maximisation.

GECE has the distinction of implementing the first DA Project in India and integrating SCADA with GIS, Asset Management Systems for Hyderabad City. We have a suite of software applications for Automation, POWERAMR®, POWERTCM®, POWEROUTAGE®, POWERDA®, POWERDMS®. We are turnkey solution providers starting from Evolution of Functional Specifications, Budget Estimates, Tender Evaluation, Project Management, Supply, Installation & Commissioning, and Post Warranty Support for systems and software for Automation Projects.

We have worked with many utilities in selecting and implementing their SCADA systems and also integrating with other applications. Our hands-on team of professionals has many years of experience successfully designing and implementing SCADA systems. Many of our consultants have worked in utility SCADA centers, in utility procurement offices and as SCADA vendors.
