Modules

6 modules in POWEREMS®.

Part of the POWER suite of workstations, available off the shelf and customised to the requirements of each utility.

01

Energy forecast

Hourly, daily and annual forecasts, and water inflow forecast.

02

System operation

Real-time power flow and optimal power flow.

03

Open access

Total transfer capability, available transfer capability (ATC) and open access.

04

Energy dispatch

Hydro power scheduling, merit order dispatch and economic dispatch.

05

Energy pricing

Nodal price, congestion, transmission price and availability based tariff (ABT).

06

Energy billing and settlement

AMR, state estimator, energy settlement, energy billing, on-line payment, accounting and energy audit.

Benefits

What POWEREMS® delivers for the utility.

  • Computation of TTC and ATC to consider approval for grant of open access
  • Implementation of open access
  • Pricing of transmission of power
  • Pricing of transmission congestion cost
