02 · City gas distribution networks

Gas

A gas distribution network shares the problems of a power network: buried assets spread across a city, customer connections to track, and meters to read and bill. GECE applies its GIS, asset management and billing capabilities to these networks.

What GECE brings

  • GPS survey and GIS mapping of pipelines, valves, regulators and customer connections
  • Network data models and conversion of legacy drawings and records
  • Asset registers with inspection and maintenance management
  • Customer connection management, metering, billing and collection

03 · Water supply utilities

Water

Water utilities need an accurate picture of their mains and connections, and a way to account for the water that leaves the treatment plant but never reaches a bill. GECE’s mapping, asset and audit approaches carry over directly.

What GECE brings

  • GIS mapping of mains, valves, hydrants, reservoirs and consumer connections
  • Asset registers and maintenance planning for pumps, pipes and storage
  • Consumer metering, billing and collection systems
  • Water audit methods, adapted from energy accounting, to locate non-revenue water

04 · Sewer networks and pumping stations

Sewerage

Sewer networks are largely out of sight, which makes reliable records essential for maintenance, complaints and expansion. GECE builds and maintains the spatial and asset data these networks depend on.

What GECE brings

  • GIS mapping of sewer lines, manholes, outfalls and pumping stations
  • Conversion of paper drawings and legacy records into a structured database
  • Asset condition records and maintenance scheduling
  • Complaint registration and field crew management

05 · Municipal bodies and planning authorities

Urban planning

Planning a city starts with an accurate base map, and every utility in it relies on the same map. GECE produces base maps from imagery and survey, and brings utility networks onto one shared platform.

What GECE brings

  • City base maps from aerial photography and satellite imagery
  • GPS and Total Station survey for detailed planning
  • Enterprise GIS with power, water, sewer and gas layers on one platform
  • Demand forecasting and infrastructure master plans for growing areas
