Power Market

Analytical support for power purchase and trading decisions.

The proliferation of IPPs in the country after promulgation of Indian Electricity Act 2003, the trading in power of the excess capacities with each generator has come into play. The gap between demand and supply necessitated a sort of platform for speculative trading in power.

GECE has human resource with many man years of experience of participation in working with IPPs, speculative buying and trading in power.

GECE does not trade energy on a speculative basis, but provides consultancy support to clients with scientific data arrived at by using analytical methods in order to help utilities in purchasing power at best prices

GECE assisted large Transco in Southern India in migrating its power trading activities smoothly to the Distribution Companies (Discos) under a multilateral funding consultancy project.

GECE also assisted the Transco Commercial Department on accessing and processing EBC (Energy Billing Center) data dealing with power trading between generating stations and Discos
