Benefits

What POWERMBC® delivers for the utility.

  • Minimizes the time between meter reading and preparation of the bill
  • Capacity to handle complex tariffs
  • Better revenue collection through on-line collections and the facility to pay where you like
  • Better customer service through prompt and correct accounting of payments
  • Prompt and automated accounting
  • Better financial management through continuous monitoring of defaulters and amounts due
  • Improved revenues and cash flows
  • Prevention of theft of energy and other malpractices
  • Load survey of customers
  • Monitoring of theft cases for disconnection, finalization and payment of assessed amounts
